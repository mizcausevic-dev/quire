How to read a listing

A short guide to prices, attributes, ownership, and the sponsored label.

Start with the category, then the price model, then the attributes that belong to that category.

Ownership is a separate fact from the provider name. If the page says ownership is not claimed, the name is only a label.

Sponsored entries say so, and they carry an end date.
